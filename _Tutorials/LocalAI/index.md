# Local AI Tutorials

Data Foundry does not only provide tools to store, modify, and collect data; it also hosts its own local AI models!

## Tutorials in this Section

* **Beginner — [Building Your First Agentic Chatbot]({% link _Tutorials/LocalAI/AgenticChatbot.md %})**: Build a personal Study Companion in ~15 minutes to see the Think-and-Act loop, memory, notes, and document search in action.
* **Advanced — [Diary Study Companion]({% link _Tutorials/LocalAI/DiaryStudyCompanion.md %})**: Design and test a full 2-week research study bot (~45 minutes) with custom `AGENTS.md` rules, structured logs, rule iteration, participant privacy, and cleanup.
* **Concepts — [AI Agents: How Agentic Chatbots Work]({% link _Learning/LocalAI/AgenticChatbots.md %})**: Understand agents, tools, the three kinds of memory, and design limits in plain language.
* If you want a quick reference to all chatbot settings, see the **[Designing Custom Chatbots]({% link _Guides/LocalAI/Chatbots.md %})** How-To Guide.
